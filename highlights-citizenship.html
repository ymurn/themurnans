February 4th, 2026, Brooklyn, NY

All:

It’s with an extremely happy heart that I welcome the newest Murnan to the family – Yinja Wen Murnan! As of 10:30 am this morning, Yinja officially became both a U.S. citizen and a Murnan. I truly hope all of you feel as proud and excited for him as we do.

It was an incredibly special day, made even more meaningful by the presence of Yinja’s good friend Marquis – his first American friend and the fabulous illustrator behind our Christmas cards, who came to celebrate this milestone with us. And of course, we marked the occasion the best way we know how: with a celebratory first meal at Han Dynasty Brooklyn, a great Chinese meal right after the ceremony.

There’s even more wonderful news to share. Yinja has recently begun co-teaching at the School of Visual Arts alongside his great mentor and friend, Sally. It really is an experience that means a lot to him. On February 9th, he will also begin his new journey as a Senior Designer at J.P. Morgan Private Bank.

It truly has been a jam packed two weeks for us and I am so happy to share all the great news going on with Yinja! I’m so happy for my Tiger, and congrats on accomplishing so many goals so quickly!

We are incredibly proud and grateful to share all of this happiness with you. Thank you for being part of our journey and for always supporting Yinja along the way.

With love and celebration,

The Murnans

Astoria, New York. Married August 6th, 2022.
