01 / Who

We met in the summer when everything stopped, but we did not stop.

“

We laugh together, we cry together, we live our best lives together.

David Murnan

June 2020, FiDi, NYC. What started as a friendship during the pandemic became 67 road trips, an apartment in Brooklyn and now Astoria, and a beautiful wedding at a barbecue joint in Gowanus.

The wedding was the middle of the story, and it has been getting better ever since. This is where we keep the rest: the falls and fire towers, the breweries and ballgames, the restaurants we tell everyone to try, and whatever we get up to next.

Married August 6th, 2022

… years, … months, and counting.

Still in love, still on the road most weekends, still finding new breweries, still keeping the table full of friends and family. Bear and Tiger, through and through. Whatever comes next lands here, so make yourself at home.
